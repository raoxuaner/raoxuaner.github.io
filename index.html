About me

XuanEr (Seona) Rao

I am an MSc student in Engineering Design & Innovation at the National University of Singapore and a Student Research Assistant in the Immersive Reality Lab. Before this, I received a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Digital Media from Macau University of Science and Technology, graduating with First-Class Honors.

My research focuses on human–computer interaction, computational design, interactive media, gamification, inclusive design, digital fabrication, and AIGC. My ongoing research explores geometry-driven motion in vacuum-actuated structures and physiological self-regulation through interactive wearables.

KNITVORTEX 2025

HCI & Game design

KNITVORTEX is a serious interactive game prototype designed for school-aged children with ADHD. The project combines rhythmic knitting activities with digital game feedback. Using sensor-equipped knitting needles, players perform physical knitting actions that are recognized by the system and translated into interactive tasks within a Unity-based 3D room, featuring room organization, to-do lists, timers, and progress-based rewards.

Learn more

EasyRide 2024

Product Design

As vehicles become increasingly digital, the loss of physical buttons alienates users who value tactile feedback and intuitive control. This project reintroduces modular physical buttons for electric vehicles, creating a safer and more inclusive driving experience for older adults and those resistant to touchscreen interfaces.

Learn more

HomeHeart 2024

Service & User Experience

HomeHeart leverages Mixed Reality (MR) technology to transform daily life for housewives, offering immersive virtual experiences that blend relaxation and enjoyment. Users can explore stunning natural landscapes, engage in interactive virtual gaming, and more, turning monotonous chores into moments of fun. By combining art and technology, HomeHeart provides an innovative way to relax, recharge, and elevate everyday routines.

Learn more

City in the Mirror 2024

Interaction Design & AIGC

This film explores Macau’s unique cultural heritage, using AI technology to breathe new life into history. By showcasing Macau’s commercial past and the evolution of Portuguese-style azulejos (ceramic tiles), it highlights the fusion of Portuguese art with traditional Chinese culture. Through vivid animated storytelling, the project aims to preserve and promote this distinctive art form, ensuring its legacy endures.
