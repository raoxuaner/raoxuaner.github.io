About me

XuanEr (Seona) Rao

I am an MSc student in Engineering Design & Innovation at the National University of Singapore and a Student Research Assistant in the Immersive Reality Lab. Before this, I received a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Digital Media from Macau University of Science and Technology, graduating with First-Class Honors and a GPA of 3.94/4.00.

My research focuses on human–computer interaction, computational design, interactive media, gamification, inclusive design, digital fabrication, and AIGC. My ongoing research explores geometry-driven motion in vacuum-actuated structures and physiological self-regulation through interactive wearables.
