Interactive wearables · Physiological self-regulation

Haptic Biofeedback Wearable

A wearable for therapist-supported self-regulation during therapeutic conversations.

RoleCo-leading
StatusOngoing
Research settingImmersive Reality Lab · NUS

Research focus

I am co-leading the design of an EDA-, PPG-, and IMU-based wearable with vibrotactile and pneumatic feedback to support physiological self-regulation and therapist-mediated co-regulation during therapeutic conversations.

Sensing and feedback

EDAPPGIMUVibrotactile feedbackPneumatic feedback
Component Channel
EDA Electrodermal activity sensing
PPG Photoplethysmography sensing
IMU Inertial sensing
Vibrotactile output Tactile feedback through vibration
Pneumatic output Tactile feedback through pneumatic actuation
