VacGuide

Programming angular motion through passive internal guide geometry.

RoleLeading
StatusOngoing
Research settingImmersive Reality Lab · NUS

Research focus

VacGuide investigates how passive internal guide geometry programs angular motion through vacuum-driven membrane collapse.

The work connects geometric design with the motion of articulated shape-changing structures, toward characterizing geometry–motion relationships that can support inverse design.

Current development

I have developed 5-mm open-guide and multi-guide primitives spanning 0–180° rotations within a shared pneumatic pouch.

Guide primitives5 mm
Rotation span0–180°
ActuationVacuum-driven membrane collapse

Ongoing direction

I am characterizing the relationship between internal guide geometry and resulting angular motion, toward inverse design of articulated shape-changing structures.
